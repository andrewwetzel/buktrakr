BukTrakr

What did you just read?

What is this?

BukTrakr is a tiny book journal. Log a book you've just read — a rating out of 10, what was good, what was bad, and anything else — and it's appended, nicely formatted, to a "BukTrakr — Book Reviews" Google Doc in your own Google Drive. Everyone who signs in gets their own doc; your reviews are yours alone.

Nothing is stored on any server: your Google connection lives in an encrypted cookie in your browser, and the app's permission only covers the one doc it creates — it cannot see the rest of your Drive. Sign in once and start logging; the title field auto-completes real books (with covers and ISBNs), a button copies your whole reading log as a prompt for any AI chat to get recommendations, and on a phone you can Add to Home Screen to use it like an app.

It's open source — view the source on GitHub or deploy your own copy.
